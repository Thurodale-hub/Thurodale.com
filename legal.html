Privacy Notice

Last updated: October 18, 2026

Who is responsible

Thurodale Inc. is responsible for personal information handled through this website. Our privacy contact is Privacy Officer, at privacy@thurodale.com.

What we collect

  • Inquiries. When you email us (for example, through the “Inquire” link), we receive what you send: typically your name, email address, organization and message.
  • Technical data. Our website host, GitHub Pages, logs each visitor’s IP address for security purposes. We do not collect other information about visitors.

Why we use it

To respond to your inquiry, to assess whether and how we can help, to keep a record of the inquiry, and to meet legal obligations.

Who else may handle it

Our email and IT service providers, and professional advisers where needed. Some of these may store or process information outside Ontario or outside Canada. We may also disclose information where the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising tools. The website loads its fonts from Google, which receives your IP address when the page loads.

Clients

If you become a client, how we handle information for the engagement is set out in your engagement agreement. The Client Login link takes you to a separate, access-controlled workspace.

How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not lead to an engagement are kept for 12 months and then deleted. Information about clients is kept as set out in the engagement agreement and as the law requires.

Your rights and complaints

You may ask to see or correct the personal information we hold about you, or raise a concern, by writing to privacy@thurodale.com. You may also contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.
